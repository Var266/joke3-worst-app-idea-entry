# CANDIDATES — The Worst App Idea Ever Pitched (re-entry)

Live entry to beat: "GhostTable lets you book a table at restaurants that closed in 2019, complete with a confirmation email, a reminder text, and a very confident maître d' who no longer exists." (174 chars) — R1 669/1852, score 533443, below the top-500 heat.

Judge criteria (in priority order):
1. Names a specific app and what it uselessly does in one sentence (meta "worst idea" framing loses).
2. Funny uselessness: absurd logic, deadpan confidence, perfect mismatch (problem nobody has).
3. Sounds like a real pitch: product name, what it does, who it is for.
4. Uses 240 chars well: one clean sentence, no padding, no second idea.
5. Vivid, specific picture, not a generic app joke.

All candidates: one sentence, ≤240 chars, product name + useless function + deadpan founder tone.

## Pairwise tournament (judge criteria order)

**Round 1**
- PerMyLastEmail vs FridgeProof: both name app + function (tie on 1). PerMyLastEmail wins on 2 — absurd logic escalates (CC boss on unsent draft, then chase a reply to nothing); FridgeProof is one-note repetition. → PerMyLastEmail
- SelfMeeting vs SighScore: tie on 1. SelfMeeting wins on 2+3 — full corporate ritual (agenda, late mark, performance review) is a perfect mismatch nobody asked for and sounds like real HR SaaS; SighScore grades one sound. → SelfMeeting
- EmptyBox vs ApplauseNow: tie on 1. EmptyBox wins on 2 — solves a problem nobody has (neighbors' opinion) with logistics (tracking, review); ApplauseNow is funny but one gag. → EmptyBox
- BarkReview vs NapReport: tie on 1. BarkReview wins on 2 — dog barks → boss performance review is a bigger category error; NapReport is cute but smaller. → BarkReview
- UnreadCast vs RentYourSpot: tie on 1. UnreadCast wins on 2+5 — "ignore them in audio form" is deadpan and vivid; RentYourSpot is a pricing joke. → UnreadCast
- CryStock vs DoorReturn: tie on 1. CryStock wins on 2 — baby cries → trades is more absurd; DoorReturn is a logistics loop. → CryStock

**Semifinals**
- PerMyLastEmail vs SelfMeeting: tie on 1, both strong on 3 (SaaS names). PerMyLastEmail wins on 2 — it creates a worse problem than it solves (your boss now waits on an email that does not exist), the purest "perfectly pointless" logic; SelfMeeting only inconveniences you. Also wins on 4 — 157 chars, tighter than 183 with no padding. → PerMyLastEmail
- EmptyBox vs BarkReview: tie on 1. EmptyBox wins on 5 — porch, Friday box, neighbors, five-star stranger is the most vivid picture in the set; BarkReview wins on absurdity but loses the picture. Criterion 2 is close, criterion 5 differs by a lot, so EmptyBox advances. → EmptyBox
- UnreadCast vs CryStock: tie on 1. UnreadCast wins on 3+4 — clear who it is for (anyone with an overflowing inbox) and one clean idea; CryStock needs the "she knew" tag to land. → UnreadCast

**Final**
- PerMyLastEmail vs EmptyBox: tie on 1. PerMyLastEmail wins on 2 (criterion 2 outranks 5) — following up hourly on a draft still in your outbox is deadpan confidence in a workflow for a problem nobody has, and it sounds like a real email-automation pitch (3). EmptyBox is more vivid (5) but its uselessness is passive; PerMyLastEmail actively makes your work life worse, which is the worse app. → PerMyLastEmail
- PerMyLastEmail vs UnreadCast: tie on 1. PerMyLastEmail wins on 2 — UnreadCast still delivers your email (in audio); PerMyLastEmail delivers an email that was never sent and demands a reply, strictly more pointless. Wins on 4 too — shorter, no second idea. → PerMyLastEmail

## Winner

**PerMyLastEmail** — "PerMyLastEmail CCs your boss on every email you never sent, then follows up hourly to ask why you have not replied to the draft still sitting in your outbox."

Why it beats the live GhostTable line: GhostTable books at closed restaurants (useless, but passive and nostalgic). PerMyLastEmail is actively harmful with founder confidence — it invents accountability for work that does not exist, names a specific SaaS-style product, states exactly what it does and who suffers (you and your boss), fits in 157 chars with one clean sentence, and leaves a vivid picture (boss CC'd, hourly follow-ups, draft still in outbox). It scores on all five criteria in order, with the strongest showing on criterion 2, which the judge weighs most after naming the app.
